# Validation Interview Guide

**Goal:** Interview 10–15 agrovet owners before writing any code.  
**Format:** In-person, 15–20 minutes, small-town Kenya (not Nairobi CBD).

---

## Screening Criteria

- Single-branch agrovet shop
- Located outside Nairobi CBD (Nakuru, Eldoret, Kisumu, Thika, Meru, etc.)
- 1–3 staff
- Owner or primary decision-maker present

---

## Questions

### Section 1: Current Invoicing Behaviour (No leading)

1. "How do you currently record a sale when a customer buys something?"
2. "Do you give customers a receipt? What does it look like?"
3. "How do you keep track of how much you've sold each day/week?"

### Section 2: eTIMS Awareness

4. "Have you heard of KRA eTIMS? Do you know what it is?"
5. "Have you registered for eTIMS? If not, why not?"
6. "Has anyone from KRA or your accountant mentioned you need to start using it?"

### Section 3: WhatsApp as a Work Tool

7. "Do you use WhatsApp for your business? How? (orders, customers, suppliers)"
8. "Do you have a separate WhatsApp number for the shop or do you use your personal one?"

### Section 4: Willingness to Use

9. "If you could text a command to issue a KRA-compliant invoice automatically and track your stock — would you use that?"
10. "What would stop you from using it?" (probes: trust, data, cost, complexity)
11. "What would you pay per month for something like this if it kept you KRA-compliant?"

---

## What to Listen For

| Signal | Validates | Kills |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| "I've been ignoring eTIMS because I don't know how" | Urgency is real | — |
| "My accountant handles it monthly" | — | May not need real-time invoicing |
| "I use WhatsApp all day for orders" | Habit fit | — |
| "I don't trust bots with my sales data" | — | Trust barrier (fixable with education) |
| "I'd pay KSh 500 for this" | Willingness to pay | — |
| "I'd need it to work offline" | — | Kills WhatsApp-first approach |
